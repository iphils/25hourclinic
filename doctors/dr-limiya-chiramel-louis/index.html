General Practitioner

Dr. Limiya Chiramel Louis

MBBS · General Practitioner

For over three decades, my approach to medicine has been guided by a simple belief: every patient deserves to be treated with absolute honesty, patience, and genuine care.

Since earning my MBBS from Calicut Medical College, I have had the privilege of serving as a General Practitioner for 33 years. My medical journey has allowed me to care for incredibly diverse communities, including several fulfilling years practicing in the Middle East, most notably at KIMS Oman Hospital in Muscat. Throughout these experiences, I have learned that while medical science is our greatest tool, true healing requires taking the time to actually listen to the person sitting in front of you.

As a doctor, I care deeply about building trust with my patients. I am particularly drawn to working with the elderly and those in palliative care. Taking the time to sit with these patients, ensuring their comfort, and providing compassionate support during their most vulnerable moments is one of the most meaningful aspects of my practice.

To me, medicine has never been just a profession; it is a lifelong commitment to guiding others through their health journeys with integrity and warmth. I look forward to welcoming you and offering you the dedicated care you deserve.
